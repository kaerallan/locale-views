How to make your view count

  1. Choose your locale and press play.
  2. Watch for 30 minutes. The timer only moves while the video is actually playing. Ads don't count.
  3. Stay on this page. Switching to another app, opening another tab or locking your phone pauses the timer. It picks up again when you come back.
  4. Keep your screen on. We'll try to keep it awake for you, but if it dims, just tap it. Plugging in your phone helps too.
  5. Use one tab only. Watching in two tabs or windows at once won't count twice.
  6. Look for "✓ view counted". That's it, thank you! Every extra 30 minutes you watch counts as another view.
